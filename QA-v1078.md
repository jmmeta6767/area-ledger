# v1078 Voice accuracy and expense OCR quality

Based on main `41278ece8531c808c56a3d4dfe742b7f7c8e1064`. Preserves the OAuth recovery, existing accounting categories, ledger formulas and storage keys.

## Changes 1–5

1. Category inference excludes the merchant phrase, so `ซื้อ ปูน 500 บาท ร้านช่างสมใจ` selects materials rather than labor. Explicit labor, rental, document and guarantee wording retain the established category values.
2. Explicit dates accept Thai/Gregorian numerals, Buddhist/Gregorian years, two-digit years, ISO dates and Thai month names/abbreviations. Calendar validation rejects nonexistent days, incomplete dates and conflicting dates. Invalid spoken dates clear the previous date and request review instead of silently reusing it.
3. Repeated-million and malformed/out-of-range money cannot become a smaller suffix or a different fee. Invalid monetary commands clear a stale amount and show a review message. Correcting amount/date removes the respective warning without rerendering the form or losing keyboard focus.
4. Added WebKit-prefixed mocked speech lifecycle coverage for silence, permission failure, stop/restart, thrown start and watchdog timeout. Browser expense QA covers the new command cases at six widths. **Physical iPhone Safari/A2HS and real speech-service quality remain untested: no physical device is connected.** Mocked speech is not a substitute for this acceptance.
5. The live provider gate now checks seven public synthetic cases: clear, faded, tilted, handwriting-style, discount/VAT, page 1 of 2 and page 2 of 2. Handwriting-style uses a rendered handwriting font; it is not a real Thai handwritten receipt. All fixtures are synthetic and contain no private receipt images.

## Incomplete documents

An explicit multi-page indicator is retained as `partialDocument`. Server and browser independently detect page markers, including Thai numerals; local OCR also applies the guard. An incomplete document cannot fill a single expense from a document-wide grand total. Multi-file results remain unselected and flagged for manual review. This is a completeness guard, not automatic merging/reconciliation of pages into a complete bill.
